Privacy Policy

App: Sudoku
Publisher: Lee Li Yun
Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information this Sudoku app (“the app”) handles when you play. The short version: your game progress stays on your device, there is no account or sign-up, and the only third party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

1. Information that stays on your device

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Puzzle progress and saved games
  • Statistics (puzzles completed, best times, streaks)
  • Your settings (theme, accent colour, timer, input mode, sound)
  • Your “Remove Ads” purchase status

We do not collect this information and we do not have access to it. It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. No account, no analytics

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device’s IP address, device and operating-system information, app usage and ad-interaction data, and device identifiers. This data is collected by Google, not by us.

On iOS, the app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, so ads are not personalized using Apple’s advertising identifier. Non-personalized ads may still use identifiers for purposes such as limiting how often you see the same ad and producing aggregated reports.

If you buy “Remove Ads”, the app no longer requests or shows ads.

Learn more about how Google handles data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Your choices in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from the “Privacy settings” option in the app’s Settings screen, where it is shown.

5. In-app purchase

“Remove Ads” is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see or receive your payment card details. Purchases can be restored on a new device using the “Restore purchases” button.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or Singapore’s PDPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you. For data collected by Google through ads, please use Google’s tools and contact channels linked above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
